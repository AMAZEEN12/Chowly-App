# Menu image sources

The demo menu image URLs were reviewed against the item names so the visuals match the menu text.

- Fried Rice — Wikimedia Commons, “Fried rice with chicken meat.jpg” (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Jollof Rice — Flawless Food, “Jollof Rice Recipe”.
- Pounded Yam & Egusi — Wikimedia Commons, “Egusi soup with pounded yam and assorted meats.jpg”.
- Zobo — Wikimedia Commons, “Chilled Zobo drink.jpg” (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Suya Platter — Tasting Table, Nigerian suya image.
- Palm Wine — Nairaland palm wine image.
- Chapman — direct image from a Nigerian Chapman recipe result.
- Catfish Pepper Soup — Nigerian pepper soup image from SISIYEMMIE.
- Grilled Fish — Nigerian grilled fish image from a Nigerian food source.
- Mocktail — non-alcoholic mocktail image from GoodEmma.
- Sparkling Water — sparkling mineral water product image.
- Malt — Malta Guinness bottle image.

Images are displayed as remote URLs rather than bundled copies. Restaurant-uploaded images are stored as data URLs and are never overwritten by the seed script.
